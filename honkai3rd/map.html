Main Campus

The Heart of St. Freya

Training Grounds

The global organization dedicated to fighting the Honkai — and the seat of its most powerful leaders.

🏛️

Central Command

The Overseer's Seat

The nerve center of Schicksal's global operations. From here, the Overseer coordinates Valkyrie deployments, research initiatives, and counter‑Honkai strategies across the world.

🪑 Overseer's Office 📊 War Room 🛡️ Council Chamber

Research & Development

The Scientific Heart

Cutting‑edge laboratories where Divine Keys, battlesuits, and anti‑Honkai technology are developed. The R&D division is responsible for some of humanity's greatest weapons.

🧪 Bio‑Lab ⚙️ Engineering Bay 📡 Observation Deck
⚠️ Classified Zone

Cathedral of the Holy See

The Sacred Ground

A place of worship and remembrance for those who fell in the battle against the Honkai. It also houses the ancient relics and records of Schicksal's long history.

🕯️ Memorial Hall 📜 Archives 🔮 Relic Vault

A scientific organization that broke away from Schicksal — now a rival force in the war against the Honkai.

🏢

ME Corp

The Brain of Anti‑Entropy

The corporate headquarters of Anti‑Entropy, housing the world's most advanced robotics and artificial intelligence research. Home to the iconic "Project Bunny" and other mecha innovations.

🤖 Robotics Lab 🧠 AI Core 📈 Corporate Office
🔧

Underground Bunker

The Last Refuge

A heavily fortified bunker deep beneath the surface, designed to survive a full‑scale Honkai eruption. It serves as a command center and safe haven for Anti‑Entropy's leaders.

🛡️ Command Bunker 💊 Medical Bay 📡 Communications Hub
⚠️ High Security

A mysterious dimension between realities — where quantum laws rule and the impossible becomes possible.

Portals to the Unknown

Unstable tears in the fabric of reality that lead into the Sea of Quanta. Only the most powerful Valkyries can traverse these rifts and return with their sanity intact.

🌀 Rift Alpha 🌀 Rift Beta 🌀 Rift Gamma
⚠️ Extreme Hazard

Bubble Worlds

Fragments of Lost Realities

Isolated pocket dimensions within the Sea of Quanta, each containing echoes of fallen worlds. They hold the memories and remnants of civilizations that were erased by the Honkai.

🌌 World 1 🌌 World 2 🌌 World 3

The Pathways Between

The winding paths that connect the Bubble Worlds to each other and to the outside world. Navigating these corridors requires immense skill and a deep understanding of quantum mechanics.

🔮 Nexus Point 🪞 Mirror Passage 🌀 Twisted Tunnel

A cosmic tree that connects all realities. Each leaf is a separate universe, and its roots delve deep into the Sea of Quanta. The Honkai is said to be a "pruning" mechanism of this tree.

🍃 Branch 1 🍃 Branch 2 🍃 Branch 3
