📖 Story of Honkai Impact 3rd

The epic saga of Valkyries, Herrschers, and the battle for humanity's future

✦ The Battle Against the Honkai ✦

In a world where humanity faces extinction from a mysterious force known as the Honkai, a select group of warriors stands between civilization and annihilation. These are the Valkyries — young women gifted with the power to fight the Honkai using advanced battlesuits and the strength of their will.

At the heart of this struggle is St. Freya Academy, where the next generation of Valkyries train to protect humanity. Among them are Kiana Kaslana, Raiden Mei, and Bronya Zaychik — three girls whose fates are intertwined with the destiny of the world itself.

This is the story of sacrifice, hope, and the unbreakable bonds between those who fight for a beautiful tomorrow.

📜 Chapter I

St. Freya Academy

The Beginning of a Journey

Kiana Kaslana arrives at St. Freya Academy, determined to become the strongest Valkyrie and protect the world. She meets Raiden Mei, a gentle girl with a hidden past, and Bronya Zaychik, a genius hacker with a mysterious connection to the Honkai.

Together, they form a team under the guidance of their instructor, Himeko Murata. But as they train and grow closer, they begin to uncover dark secrets about the Honkai — and about themselves. The peaceful days at the academy are numbered, and the true battle is just beginning.

🏫 St. Freya Academy
📜 Chapter II

The Herrscher of Thunder

Mei's Awakening

The first major crisis erupts when Raiden Mei awakens as the Herrscher of Thunder — a being of immense power controlled by the Honkai. Kiana and Bronya must confront their friend and find a way to save her without losing themselves in the process.

The battle forces the team to confront the reality of the Honkai: that the greatest threat may come from within. But through their bond, they prove that love and friendship can overcome even the darkest forces.

⚡ Nagazora · Herrscher Awakening
📜 Chapter III

Schicksal's Secrets

The Overseer and the Truth

The team is drawn into the machinations of Schicksal, the global organization tasked with fighting the Honkai. Under the leadership of Overseer Otto Apocalypse, Schicksal's true nature is revealed — a web of manipulation, sacrifice, and ancient secrets.

Himeko and Kiana uncover shocking truths about the origin of the Honkai and the role of the Herrschers. The battle is no longer just about survival — it's about the very fate of humanity.

🏛️ Schicksal Headquarters
📜 Chapter IV

Anti-Entropy

The Enemy of My Enemy

As Schicksal's shadow grows, the team finds an unlikely ally in Anti-Entropy — a rival organization of scientists and engineers who oppose Schicksal's methods. Led by Welt Yang, the founder of Anti-Entropy, they offer a different path.

But old wounds run deep, and the alliance is fragile. Meanwhile, a new threat emerges: the Herrscher of the Void, a being of pure destruction, threatens to consume everything. Kiana must confront her darkest fears and embrace her true power.

🤖 Anti-Entropy · ME Corp
📜 Chapter V

The Sea of Quanta

Between Worlds

The team ventures into the Sea of Quanta, a dimension of infinite possibilities where the rules of reality don't apply. Here, they encounter Seele Vollerei, a mysterious girl trapped between worlds, and confront the Herrscher of Reason.

The Sea of Quanta reveals the truth about the Honkai's origins and the existence of the Imaginary Tree. It's a journey that will change everything they thought they knew about the world — and themselves.

🌊 Sea of Quanta · Bubble Worlds
📜 Chapter VI

The Herrscher of Sentience

Fu Hua's Legacy

Fu Hua, the team's enigmatic mentor, reveals her true identity as the Herrscher of Sentience. Her past as a warrior from a forgotten age comes to light, and she must decide where her loyalties lie — with the past or with the future.

The battle against the Herrscher of Sentience tests the bonds of the team like never before. It's a story of redemption, sacrifice, and the power of memories to shape who we become.

🐉 Fu Hua · Herrscher of Sentience
📜 Chapter VII

Elysium Everlasting

The Realm of the Flame‑Chasers

The team enters Elysium Everlasting, a digital realm created by the Flame‑Chasers — ancient warriors who fought the Honkai in a previous era. Led by the enigmatic Elysia, they learn of the sacrifices made in the past to protect the future.

This chapter reveals the origins of the Herrschers and the true nature of the Honkai. It's a story of humanity's resilience and the eternal struggle against the forces of destruction.

🦋 Elysium Everlasting · Elysia
📜 Chapter VIII · Coming Soon

The Final Battle

The Moon · The Finality

The story reaches its climax on the Moon, where the Herrscher of Finality awaits. Kiana, now the Herrscher of Flamescion, must confront her destiny and make the ultimate sacrifice to protect everyone she loves.

The finale of Honkai Impact 3rd's main story is a testament to the power of hope, friendship, and the unyielding human spirit. It's the end of one journey — and the beginning of another.

🌙 Moon Base · Herrscher of Finality
