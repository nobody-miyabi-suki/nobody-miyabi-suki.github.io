🗺️ Map of the Cosmos

Explore the worlds of the Astral Express

🚂

Astral Express

The Star-Sailing Train

The Astral Express is a train that travels through the stars, invented by the Aeon of Trailblaze, Akivili. It serves as the home base for the Trailblazer and the crew, and is the primary mode of transportation between worlds.

🚃 Parlor Car 🛋️ Guest Cabin 📚 Archives 🌌 Observation Deck
🌟 The Nameless · Akivili
🛸

Herta Space Station

The Museum Among the Stars

The Herta Space Station is the first location players visit. Founded by Herta, a member of The Genius Society, it is a vast space museum filled with countless Curios and a research base. It is home to researchers, the "Simulated Universe," and is where the Trailblazer's journey begins.

🎛️ Master Control Zone 🏗️ Base Zone 📦 Supply Zone 🗄️ Storage Zone 🔮 Seclusion Zone
🧠 Genius Society · Herta
❄️

Jarilo-VI

The Planet of Eternal Freeze

Jarilo-VI is the first actual world visited by the Astral Express. A frozen planet encased in an "Eternal Freeze" caused by a Stellaron. The only safe haven is the city of Belobog, divided into the Overworld and Underworld.

🏛️ Administrative District 🌨️ Outlying Snow Plains ⚔️ Silvermane Guard Restricted Zone 🌫️ Corridor of Fading Echoes ⛰️ Everwinter Hill 🪨 Boulder Town ⛏️ Great Mine 🏚️ Rivet Town 🤖 Robot Settlement
❄️ Eternal Freeze · Stellaron
🌿

The Xianzhou Luofu

The Flagship of the Xianzhou Alliance

The Xianzhou Luofu is one of the six flagships of the Xianzhou Alliance. It sails through the galaxy like a one-way arrow, with the goal of eradicating the Denizens of Abundance. It is the third accessible world.

⛵ Central Starskiff Haven ☁️ Cloudford 🌌 Stargazer Navalia 🛸 Aurum Alley 🏯 Palace of Astrum 🌳 Scalegorge Waterscape ⚔️ Alchemy Commission 🔮 Divination Commission 🌲 Arbor
🎯 The Hunt · Lan
🌙

Penacony

The Planet of Festivities

Penacony is the fourth accessible world. Also known as the "Planet of Festivities," it is located in the Asdana star system. Once an IPC frontier prison, it is now a luxurious dreamscape hotel where guests can experience the Sweetdream Paradise.

🏨 The Reverie (Hotel) 🌅 Moment of Daybreak 🌄 Moment of Scorchsand ✨ Moment of Stars 💧 Moment of Morning Dew 🧊 Moment of Sol 🎠 Dreamscape
💤 The Family · Memoria
🏛️

Amphoreus

The Eternal Land

Amphoreus, also known as the Eternal Land, is the fifth accessible world. It is an isolated "sandbox world" created by Scepter δ-me13. A world of ancient ruins and mysteries.

🏛️ Okhema ⛏️ Old Quarry Ground 🏰 Castrum Kremnos 🌌 Evernight 🌅 Dawn 🌍 Outworld 🌿 Exotale
⚙️ Scepter · Sandbox
🎭

Planarcadia

The World of Elation

Planarcadia is a civilization on Ahatopia, a world covered in the Elation. It is Himeko's homeland and the place where the Astral Express once crashed.

🏙️ Duomension City 📚 Graphia Academy 🌆 Dovebrook District 🍻 "World's End" Tavern
🎉 The Elation · Aha
