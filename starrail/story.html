📖 Story of the Cosmos

The epic journey of the Trailblazer across the stars

✦ The Trailblazer's Journey ✦

In a universe where gods known as Aeons govern the paths of existence, a mysterious being known as the Stellaron — a "Cancer of All Worlds" — threatens to consume entire planets. You are the Trailblazer, a unique individual who can host a Stellaron without being destroyed.

After awakening on the Herta Space Station, you join the Astral Express — a star-sailing train that travels through the cosmos following the path of the Aeon Akivili. Alongside your companions — Dan Heng, March 7th, Welt, and Himeko — you embark on a journey to stabilize Stellarons across different worlds, uncovering the secrets of the Aeons, the Stellaron Hunters, and the fate of the universe.

This is the story of the Trailblazer's search for the truth — and the path they choose to walk.

📜 Prologue

The Awakening

Herta Space Station · The Beginning

The Trailblazer awakens on the Herta Space Station with no memories of their past. They are immediately thrust into chaos as the space station is attacked by the Anti-Matter Legion, led by Doomsday Beast. Guided by March 7th and Dan Heng, they discover they can control a Stellaron within them.

After a fierce battle, they meet Herta, the station's owner, and Welt, a seasoned traveler. They are invited to join the Astral Express, setting the stage for their interstellar journey. However, a mysterious figure — Kafka of the Stellaron Hunters — seems to know about the Trailblazer's past and purpose.

🛸 Herta Space Station
📜 Chapter I

The Eternal Freeze

Jarilo-VI · The Planet Under Ice

The Astral Express arrives at Jarilo-VI, a frozen planet covered in "Eternal Freeze." The only city is Belobog, divided between the Overworld and Underworld. The Trailblazer discovers that a Stellaron is the source of the eternal winter, buried deep in the planet's core.

With the help of the Silvermane Guards and the resistance fighters of the Underworld, the Trailblazer ventures into the Everwinter Hill and confronts the Stellaron. In a climactic battle, they purify the Stellaron, saving Belobog from total destruction. The city begins to thaw, and the Trailblazer earns the gratitude of its people.

❄️ Jarilo-VI · Belobog
📜 Chapter II

The Xianzhou Luofu

The Flagship of the Alliance

The Express receives a distress call from the Xianzhou Luofu, one of the six flagships of the Xianzhou Alliance. The Luofu is under attack by the Abundance — an ancient enemy of the Alliance. The Trailblazer learns about the conflict between The Hunt (Aeon Lan) and Abundance (Aeon Yaoshi).

The Trailblazer uncovers a conspiracy involving the Stellaron Hunters and the secret of the Ambrosial Arbor. They join forces with Jing Yuan, the general of the Luofu's Cloud Knights, and Fu Xuan, the master diviner, to prevent the resurrection of a powerful Abundance entity. The story concludes with a tense standoff, and the Trailblazer earns the respect of the Alliance.

🌿 Xianzhou Luofu
📜 Chapter III

The Sweetdream Paradise

Penacony · The Planet of Festivities

The Astral Express arrives at Penacony, a luxurious dreamscape hotel planet. Once a prison, it is now a grand resort where guests can experience the Sweetdream Paradise — a realm where dreams become reality. However, beneath the festivities lies a dark secret.

The Trailblazer uncovers the truth about The Family, the rulers of Penacony, and their manipulation of Memoria (the fabric of dreams). They meet Firefly, a mysterious companion with ties to the Stellaron Hunters, and confront the Death meme. In the end, the Trailblazer must choose between the dream and reality, facing the Aeon of Remembrance (Fuli).

🌙 Penacony · The Dreamscape
📜 Chapter IV

The Eternal Land

Amphoreus · The Sandbox World

The Express journeys to Amphoreus, also known as the Eternal Land. This world is isolated from the rest of the universe, a "sandbox" created by an ancient Scepter. The Trailblazer discovers the secrets of the Giants and the Scepter δ-me13.

The story delves into the origins of the Aeons and the cycle of destruction and creation. The Trailblazer must navigate ancient ruins, face challenges from the Darkness, and uncover the truth about the world's isolation. The chapter reveals crucial lore about the universe's history.

🏛️ Amphoreus · The Sandbox
📜 Chapter V · Coming Soon

The World of Elation

Planarcadia · Himeko's Homeland

The Express arrives at Planarcadia, a world covered in the Elation of Aha. This is the homeland of Himeko, the navigator of the Astral Express. The Trailblazer learns about the crash of the Astral Express on this planet and the events that shaped Himeko's past.
