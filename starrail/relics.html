🧩 Relics & Planar Ornaments

All relic sets and planar ornaments to optimize your characters

🗡️ Cavern of Corrosion · Relic Sets

Relic sets provide powerful stat bonuses and unique effects. Each set comes in a 2-piece and 4-piece variant. Below are all the key sets available in Honkai Star Rail.

🌾

Musketeer of Wild Wheat

★★★★★
2-Piece ATK +12%
4-Piece Increases Basic Attack DMG by 20%. When using Basic Attack, SPD +8% for 1 turn.
📌 Cavern of Corrosion 👤 Basic Attack DPS
🛡️

Knight of Purity Palace

★★★★★
2-Piece DEF +15%
4-Piece Increases the DMG absorbed by the Shield created by the wearer by 20%.
📌 Cavern of Corrosion 🛡️ Shielders
❄️

Hunter of Glacial Forest

★★★★★
2-Piece Ice DMG +10%
4-Piece After using Ultimate, increases the wearer's CRIT DMG by 25% for 2 turns.
📌 Cavern of Corrosion ❄️ Ice DPS
💫

Thief of Shooting Meteor

★★★★★
2-Piece Break Effect +16%
4-Piece Increases the wearer's Break Effect by 16%. When the wearer breaks an enemy, restores 3 Energy.
📌 Cavern of Corrosion 💥 Break Specialists
🥊

Champion of Streetwise Boxing

★★★★★
2-Piece Physical DMG +10%
4-Piece After entering battle or using attack, increases ATK by 5% and can stack up to 5 times.
📌 Cavern of Corrosion 💪 Physical DPS
🔥

Firesmith of Lava-Forging

★★★★★
2-Piece Fire DMG +10%
4-Piece Increases the wearer's Skill DMG by 12%. After using Ultimate, increases Fire DMG by 20% for 1 turn.
📌 Cavern of Corrosion 🔥 Fire DPS
✨

Genius of Brilliant Stars

★★★★★
2-Piece Quantum DMG +10%
4-Piece Increases DEF penetration by 10% and max stack 3.
📌 Cavern of Corrosion 🌌 Quantum DPS
🦅

Eagle of Twilight Line

★★★★★
2-Piece Wind DMG +10%
4-Piece After the wearer uses Ultimate, their turn is advanced forward by 25%.
📌 Cavern of Corrosion 🌪️ Wind DPS
❄️

Guard of Wuthering Snow

★★★★★
2-Piece Reduces DMG taken by 8%.
4-Piece At the beginning of each turn, if the wearer's HP is below 50%, heals 20% of their max HP.
📌 Cavern of Corrosion 🛡️ Tank/Sustain
⚡

Band of Sizzling Thunder

★★★★★
2-Piece Lightning DMG +10%
4-Piece Increases the wearer's ATK by 20% for 1 turn after using Skill.
📌 Cavern of Corrosion ⚡ Lightning DPS

🌀 Planar Ornaments

Planar Ornaments are special 2-piece sets obtained from the Simulated Universe. They provide unique effects and are essential for end-game builds.

🌌

Inert Salsotto

★★★★★
2-Piece Increases CRIT Rate by 8%. When current CRIT Rate is 50% or higher, increases Ultimate and Follow-up attack DMG by 15%.
🎮 Simulated Universe · World 3 🎯 Crit DPS
🛸

Space Sealing Station

★★★★★
2-Piece Increases ATK by 12%. When SPD reaches 120 or higher, ATK increased by an additional 12%.
🎮 Simulated Universe · World 1 📈 ATK Scaler
⏳

Fleet of the Ageless

★★★★★
2-Piece Increases Max HP by 12%. When SPD reaches 120 or higher, increases all allies' ATK by 8%.
🎮 Simulated Universe · World 2 🩸 HP Scaler
🏴‍☠️

Talia: Kingdom of Banditry

★★★★★
2-Piece Increases Break Effect by 16%. When SPD reaches 145 or higher, additionally increases Break Effect by 20%.
🎮 Simulated Universe · World 5 💥 Break Effect
💼

Pan-Cosmic Commercial Enterprise

★★★★★
2-Piece Increases the wearer's Effect Hit Rate by 10%. ATK is increased by 25% of Effect Hit Rate.
🎮 Simulated Universe · World 6 🎯 Debuffer
🏛️

Belobog of the Architects

★★★★★
2-Piece Increases DEF by 15%. When Effect Hit Rate reaches 50% or higher, additionally increases DEF by 15%.
🎮 Simulated Universe · World 4 🛡️ Defense Shielder
💨

Sprightly Vonwacq

★★★★★
2-Piece Increases Energy Regeneration Rate by 5%. When SPD reaches 120 or higher, the wearer's turn is advanced forward by 40% at the start of battle.
🎮 Simulated Universe · World 7 ⚡ Energy Support
🐺

Duran, Dynasty of Running Wolves

★★★★★
2-Piece Increases Follow-Up Attack DMG by 25%. When allies use Follow-Up attacks, gains a stack of "Merit" (max 5) increasing ATK by 5%.
🎮 Simulated Universe · World 8 ⚔️ Follow-Up Attack
⛩️

Izumo Gensei & Takama Divine Realm

★★★★★
2-Piece When an ally in the team is of the same element, ATK increased by 12%. If two or more allies are of the same element, additionally increases CRIT Rate by 8%.
🎮 Simulated Universe · World 9 ⚔️ Mono-Element
