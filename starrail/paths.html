🌀 Paths of the Aeons

The seven (and more) paths that define the destiny of every character

In Honkai Star Rail, each character follows a Path that determines their combat role and synergy with the Aeons. These paths dictate the core mechanics of the character, from dealing massive damage to protecting allies or healing the team.

🌀

Destruction

Aeon of Destruction · Nanook
High damage dealers with a balance between single-target and area attacks. Often have abilities that consume or manipulate HP for devastating effects. They are resilient front-liners.
⚔️ Playstyle: Blast & HP Manipulation
Blade Dan Heng IL Firefly Yunli Clara Hook Arlan
⚔️ Blast DPS Berserker Style
🎯

The Hunt

Aeon of The Hunt · Lan
Single-target specialists who excel at dealing massive damage to a single enemy. They are fast, critical-hit oriented, and often manipulate turn order to take down high-priority threats.
🎯 Playstyle: Single-Target & Speed
Seele Topaz Dr. Ratio Yanqing Sushang Dan Heng
🎯 Single-Target DPS Crit & Speed
📚

Erudition

Aeon of Erudition · Nous
Masters of Area of Effect (AoE) damage. Erudition characters are the best at clearing waves of enemies and dealing with multiple targets simultaneously. They are often strategic and intelligence-based.
📚 Playstyle: AoE & Cleave
Jing Yuan Argenti Herta Qingque Serval Rappa
📚 AoE DPS Wave Clearer
🎵

Harmony

Aeon of Harmony · Xipe
Supreme supports that boost the entire team's combat effectiveness. They provide offensive buffs (ATK, DMG, Crit), action advance, and energy regeneration to enable their allies.
🎵 Playstyle: Buffs & Action Advance
Bronya Sparkle Ruan Mei Robin Tingyun Asta Yukong
🎵 Amplifier Team Booster
🌌

Nihility

Aeon of Nihility · IX
Debuffers and DoT (Damage over Time) specialists. Nihility characters weaken enemies by reducing their DEF, ATK, or applying elemental DoTs like Burn, Shock, or Wind Shear that deal damage over multiple turns.
🌌 Playstyle: Debuffs & DoT
Kafka Acheron Black Swan Welt Pela Sampo Guinaifen
🌌 Debuffer/DoT Enemy Weakening
🛡️

Preservation

Aeon of Preservation · Qlipoth
Defensive tanks and shielders. Preservation characters protect the team by providing powerful shields, increasing DEF, taunting enemies, and absorbing damage to keep the team alive.
🛡️ Playstyle: Shields & Taunt
Aventurine Fu Xuan Gepard March 7th Trailblazer (Fire)
🛡️ Shielder/Tank Protector
🌿

Abundance

Aeon of Abundance · Yaoshi
Dedicated healers and cleansers. Abundance characters restore HP to allies, remove debuffs, and sometimes provide minor offensive buffs or damage. They are the backbone of any high-difficulty team.
🌿 Playstyle: Healing & Cleansing
Luocha Huohuo Bailu Natasha Lynx Gallagher
🌿 Healer Sustain
❄️

Remembrance

Aeon of Remembrance · Fuli
New Path introduced in Version 3.0. Focuses on Freezing enemies and summoning creatures. Characters of this path excel at controlling the battlefield through memory and ice.
❄️ Playstyle: Freeze & Summon
The Herta Aglaea (More coming soon)
