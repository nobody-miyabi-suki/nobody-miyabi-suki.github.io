Kafka

The Elegant Stellaron Hunter

A member of the mysterious Stellaron Hunters, Kafka is an elegant and enigmatic woman who wields lightning with deadly precision. Her calm demeanor and calculated actions make her one of the most feared and respected figures in the cosmos.

As a master of the Nihility path, she specializes in applying Shock and triggering devastating follow-up attacks that leave her enemies paralyzed. Her presence on the battlefield is as captivating as it is lethal.

Biography · Kafka

"The universe is full of surprises. I intend to enjoy every single one."

Kafka is a member of the Stellaron Hunters, a group of enigmatic individuals who operate outside the laws of the universe. Her past is shrouded in mystery, but her reputation precedes her — she is a woman of unparalleled skill and chilling composure.

Her powers are a testament to her connection to the Nihility path. She can weave lightning into her attacks, paralyzing enemies and leaving them vulnerable to devastating follow-up strikes. Her calm and calculated demeanor makes her a formidable opponent, as she never acts without purpose.

Despite her fearsome reputation, Kafka is not without her own code of ethics. She often appears to help those in need — though always on her own terms. Her true motivations remain a mystery, but her loyalty to the Stellaron Hunters is unwavering.
