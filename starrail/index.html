Kafka · Stellaron Hunter

A member of the Stellaron Hunters, Kafka is a mysterious and elegant woman who wields lightning with deadly precision. Her calm demeanor and calculated actions make her one of the most feared and respected figures in the cosmos.

As a master of the Nihility path, she specializes in applying shock and triggering devastating follow-up attacks that leave her enemies paralyzed.
