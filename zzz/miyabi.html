Biography · Miyabi

"The Hollows fear my blade. And so should you."

Miyabi is a legendary Void Hunter, one of the most elite warriors in New Eridu. Her reputation precedes her — a warrior of unmatched skill and unwavering resolve, she is feared by both enemies and allies alike.

Born with eyes that burn like crimson fire, Miyabi possesses the rare ability to perceive the true nature of the Hollows. Her katana, forged from the very essence of Hollow energy, is said to be one of the most powerful weapons in existence.

Despite her fearsome reputation, Miyabi fights with a purpose: to protect the innocent and ensure that the Hollows never claim another life. She is a silent guardian, a watchful protector, and a force of nature that even the most powerful Ethereals dare not challenge.
