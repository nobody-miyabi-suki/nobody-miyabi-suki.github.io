🤖 Bangboo Companions

Your adorable robotic partners in the Hollows · Manufactured by the Bangboo Manufacturing Association (BMA)

These quirky little machines are more than just cute faces. Bangboo assist Proxies in combat, exploration, and even provide moral support during the darkest Hollow dives.

★ S-Rank
Amillion
🛡️ Support 💰 Wealth
A star in the Hollows, Amillion is obsessed with money and carries a giant coin. It boosts team energy and provides economic buffs to its allies.
⚡ Skill: Coin Toss · Energy Regen
★ S-Rank
Butler
💎 Support 🍵 Heal
Elegant and refined, Butler serves tea and healing to agents in need. Its sophisticated AI allows it to predict when a team member needs a boost.
⚡ Skill: Royal Service · Party Heal
★ S-Rank
Sharkboo
⚔️ Attack 💧 Water
Aggressive and territorial, Sharkboo chomps through Ethereals with its razor-sharp teeth. It's the perfect front-line combat buddy.
⚡ Skill: Jaws of the Hollow · Melee Burst
★ S-Rank
Resonaboo
💎 Support 🌀 Elemental
A mystical Bangboo that harnesses elemental resonance. Resomaboo amplifies the elemental damage of your agents, turning the tide of battle.
⚡ Skill: Elemental Harmony · Buff
★ A-Rank
Bangvolver
⚔️ Attack 🎯 Ranged
Dual-wielding pistols, Bangvolver is a gunslinger through and through. It provides consistent ranged fire support to keep enemies at bay.
⚡ Skill: Gunpowder Waltz · Ranged Barrage
★ A-Rank
Cryboo
⚔️ Attack ❄️ Ice
Freezing the battlefield one step at a time. Cryboo launches icicles at enemies, slowing them down and dealing consistent Ice damage.
⚡ Skill: Frost Bolt · Ice Damage
★ A-Rank
Electroboo
⚔️ Attack ⚡ Shock
A high-voltage companion, Electroboo chains lightning between enemies, making it perfect for dealing with groups of Ethereals.
⚡ Skill: Chain Lightning · AoE Shock
★ B-Rank
Avocaboo
💎 Support 🍃 Heal
Don't let the healthy appearance fool you; Avocaboo packs a punch while keeping the team nourished and healed. A reliable all-rounder.
⚡ Skill: Guac Shield · Small Heal
★ B-Rank
Explorerboo
🛠️ Utility 🔍 Scouting
The ultimate scout, Explorerboo can detect hidden passages and resources within the Hollows. It's the perfect companion for loot hunters.
⚡ Skill: Pathfinder · Reveals Secrets
★ B-Rank
Safety
🛡️ Defense 🔰 Shield
Safety is a protective Bangboo that erects barriers around its team. It might be small, but it takes its job of keeping Proxies safe very seriously.
⚡ Skill: Bubble Barrier · Shield
★ A-Rank
Luckyboo
💎 Support 🎲 Luck
A mysterious Bangboo that seems to bend probability. Luckyboo increases the drop rates of rare materials and boosts the team's critical hit rate.
⚡ Skill: Fortune's Favor · Luck Buff
