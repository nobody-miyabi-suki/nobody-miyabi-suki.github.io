Biography · Ellen Joe

"Elegance is a weapon. And I wield it perfectly."

Ellen Joe is a maid of the prestigious Victoria Housekeeping organization in New Eridu. Known for her icy demeanor and razor-sharp skills, she is one of the most capable agents in the city.

Despite her cold exterior, Ellen harbors a deep sense of loyalty to her comrades and a fierce determination to protect the innocent. Her ice-bladed weapons are not just tools of combat — they are extensions of her very being.

Ellen's past remains shrouded in mystery, but her reputation as a maid who cleans up the messes of the Hollows with deadly precision is known throughout New Eridu. Those who underestimate her elegant appearance soon find themselves frozen in place — permanently.
