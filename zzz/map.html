🗺️ New Eridu & The Hollows

Explore the last bastion of civilization and the dangerous zones that surround it

🏙️ Districts of New Eridu

The beating heart of humanity's last city — where proxies, agents, and civilians live, work, and fight to survive.

🏘️

Sixth Street

The Proxies' Hub

A bustling commercial district filled with arcades, shops, and the iconic Random Play video store. This is where the story begins and where proxies take on their most dangerous commissions.

🛍️ Shops 🎮 Arcade 📼 Random Play ☕ Coffee Shop
🏛️

Lumina Square

The Heart of the City

The central plaza of New Eridu, surrounded by towering skyscrapers and government buildings. It's a place of grand speeches, public events, and the constant presence of the PubSec and Hollow Observation Data headquarters.

🏛️ Government 👮 PubSec 📊 H.O.D. 🗳️ Events
⛵

Port Elpis

Gateway to the Outer Ring

A sprawling industrial port connecting New Eridu to the lawless Outer Ring. Smuggling, trade, and mercenary work thrive here, making it a hotspot for those looking to make a name (or a fortune) outside the city's strict rules.

🚢 Harbor 🔧 Industry 🌆 Outer Ring 💼 Black Market
📡

Hollow Observation Data

The Eye of the Storm

The central research and monitoring facility for all Hollow activity. Scientists and analysts work around the clock to predict Hollow expansions, study Ethereals, and provide critical intel to the agents on the ground.

🔬 Research 🖥️ Monitoring 📈 Data Analysis ⚠️ Early Warning

🌆 The Hollows

These are the corrupted zones where reality twists, Ethereals roam, and the most valuable resources lie. Only the bravest (or most desperate) proxies dare to enter.

☣️

Hollow Zero

Ground Zero of the Cataclysm

The birthplace of the Hollows. This is the most dangerous and unpredictable zone, where the Ethereals are at their strongest. Only the most elite squads (like the Void Hunters) are permitted to enter.

☣️ Extreme Danger 👾 Elite Ethereals 🔮 Mystery 💀 High Risk
⚠️ S-Rank Only · Extreme Hazard
🏚️

The Chasm

The Ruined Underground

A vast underground Hollow that collapsed into a massive sinkhole. It's a labyrinth of ruined buildings, abandoned metro lines, and terrifying Ethereals that thrive in the darkness. Perfect for resource extraction — if you survive.

🏚️ Ruins 🚇 Metro 💎 Resources 🌑 Dark Zone
⚠️ High Risk · A-Rank+
🎭

Ballet Twins Road

The Haunted Theater District

Once a glamorous entertainment hub, this Hollow is now frozen in a perpetual performance. Ethereals here mimic dancers and actors, making it both beautiful and deadly. A favorite hunting ground for agents who appreciate the macabre.

🎭 Theater 💃 Mimicry 🎵 Ambient Music 🌙 Twisted Reality
⚠️ Mid Risk · A-Rank+
⚔️

Rusted Blade

The Mercenary Training Ground

An industrial Hollow filled with abandoned factories and military equipment. Ethereals here are aggressive and combat-oriented, making it the perfect proving ground for new agents. The atmosphere is charged with the echoes of old battles.

🏭 Factories 🤖 Combat Ethereals ⚡ Electric Hazards 💥 Training Ground
⚠️ Mid Risk · A-Rank+
🪹

Khors' Nest

The Outer Ring Frontier

Located on the outskirts of the Outer Ring, this Hollow is a tangled web of vegetation and ruined structures. It's home to unique, nature-based Ethereals and is a key location for proxy commissions involving exploration and wildlife study.

🌳 Overgrown 🐾 Nature Ethereals 🌆 Outer Ring 🧪 Exploration
⚠️ Mid Risk · A-Rank+
