🗺️ Map of Teyvat

Explore the seven nations and their unique landscapes

🏔️

Mondstadt

The City of Freedom

Nestled among the mountains and vast plains, Mondstadt is the home of the Anemo Archon Barbatos. Known for its windmills, wine, and the legendary Knights of Favonius.

🌪️ Anemo · Freedom
🌸

Liyue

The City of Contracts

A prosperous harbor city built upon the remains of an ancient god. Ruled by the Geo Archon Rex Lapis, it is the heart of commerce and tradition in Teyvat.

⛰️ Geo · Contracts
⚡

Inazuma

The City of Eternity

An archipelago of thunderous storms and cherry blossoms. Governed by the Raiden Shogun and the Electro Archon, it is a land of duality — beauty and peril.

⚡ Electro · Eternity
🌳

Sumeru

The City of Wisdom

Home to the Akademiya, Sumeru is a lush rainforest and vast desert. Guided by the Dendro Archon, it is the center of knowledge and scholarship in Teyvat.

🌿 Dendro · Wisdom
💧

Fontaine

The City of Justice

A nation of water, art, and technology. Ruled by the Hydro Archon, Fontaine is known for its opera, advanced machinery, and the pursuit of justice above all.

💧 Hydro · Justice
🔥

Natlan

The City of War

The land of fire and dragons. Natlan is a warrior nation where the Pyro Archon commands the strongest fighters. Its culture is built on strength and honor.

🔥 Pyro · War
❄️

Snezhnaya

The City of Love

A frozen tundra ruled by the Cryo Archon and the Fatui. Snezhnaya is the most formidable nation in Teyvat, known for its military might and deep secrets.

❄️ Cryo · Love
